## Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

It Takes Two is an acclaimed cooperative action-adventure game developed by Electronic Arts Inc. It revolves around Cody and May, a married couple facing challenges in their relationship that lead them toward divorce. When their daughter, Rose, creates two rag dolls resembling her parents, the couple is magically transported into these dolls, sparking a unique adventure filled with challenges and teamwork. Unlike typical single-player games, It Takes Two thrives on collaboration, making it a compelling experience for players who enjoy cooperative gameplay.
